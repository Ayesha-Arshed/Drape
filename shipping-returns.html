Shipping & Returns

Everything you need to know about how your order gets to you, and what happens if it doesn't work out.

Home / Shipping & Returns

Shipping

We aim to get your order to you as quickly and safely as possible, wherever you are in Pakistan.

  • Free Delivery

    All orders ship free, no minimum spend required.

  • Delivery Time

    Orders typically arrive within 3–5 business days after dispatch.

  • Order Tracking

    You'll receive tracking details by email or SMS as soon as your order ships.

  • Delivery Areas

    We currently deliver across Pakistan. International shipping is coming soon.

  • Premium Packaging

    Every order arrives in Drape's signature packaging, ready to gift or keep.

Returns

Not quite right? We make returns and exchanges simple.

  • 7-Day Return Window

    Returns are accepted within 7 days of delivery.

  • Item Condition

    Items must be unworn, unwashed, and returned with original tags and packaging.

  • How to Start a Return

    Go to your order history and select "Request Return", or contact our support team.

  • Exchanges

    Size or color exchanges are available subject to stock availability.

  • Refunds

    Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days of receiving the returned item.

Still have questions? Check our FAQ page or get in touch — we're happy to help.
