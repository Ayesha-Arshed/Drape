SUPPORT

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about orders, shipping, and returns.

Home / FAQ

Orders

How do I place an order?

Simply browse our collections, add your chosen pieces to the cart, and follow the checkout steps. You'll receive a confirmation once your order is placed.

Can I change or cancel my order after placing it?

If your order hasn't been processed yet, contact us as soon as possible and we'll do our best to accommodate changes or cancellations.

Do you offer gift wrapping?

Yes, all our pieces come in premium packaging by default, and gift wrapping can be requested at checkout.

Shipping

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery typically takes 3–5 business days. You'll receive tracking details as soon as your order ships.

Do you offer free delivery?

Yes, we offer free delivery on all orders, no minimum spend required.

Which areas do you deliver to?

We currently deliver across Pakistan. International shipping options are coming soon.

Returns & Exchanges

What is your return policy?

We offer easy returns within 7 days of delivery, provided items are unworn, unwashed, and in original packaging with tags attached.

How do I start a return?

Go to your order history and select "Request Return", or contact our support team directly and we'll guide you through the process.

Can I exchange an item for a different size?

Yes, exchanges are available subject to stock. Reach out to our support team with your order number to arrange it.

Support

How can I contact customer support?

Our team is available 24/7 through the Contact page, or you can reach us via our social channels linked in the footer.

Do you have a physical store?

At the moment, Drape operates exclusively online, allowing us to bring you the best prices and a wider selection.
